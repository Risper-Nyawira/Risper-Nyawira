# Hi, I'm Risper Nyawira 👋

### Agricultural Research | Data Analytics | Sustainable Food Systems

I work at the intersection of agricultural economics, applied research, data analysis, and sustainable development. My goal is to turn agricultural, market, climate, and programme data into evidence that helps inform better decisions for farmers, communities, and development practitioners.

- 🎓 **Background:** Agricultural Economics and Rural Development, JKUAT
- 🌱 **Research interests:** Food security, agricultural markets, climate resilience, and sustainable food systems
- 📊 **Analytics:** SQL, SQLite, Python, pandas, Matplotlib, Power BI, and Excel
- 🔎 **Development practice:** Research, data quality assurance, value-chain analysis, and MEAL
- 🚀 **Current focus:** Building reproducible agricultural research projects and decision-support tools

## My Approach

- Ask practical, decision-relevant research questions.
- Document data sources, cleaning steps, and limitations.
- Make analysis reproducible and results understandable.
- Distinguish correlation from causation.
- Translate evidence into useful insights for agriculture and development.
